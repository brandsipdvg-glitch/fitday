How it works

From Search To Check-In

Six steps. Nothing changes at your front desk except more footfall.

FAQ

Questions Gym Owners Ask

Do I need to change my memberships?

No. Your existing memberships stay exactly the same. Day passes sit alongside them as a separate, additional product.

Can I set my own day-pass price?

Yes. You decide what users pay. You can change your price at any time from the dashboard.

Do I get paid automatically?

Yes. Settlements are calculated every Monday to Sunday week and transferred directly to your bank account, without you chasing anyone.

Is there any upfront cost?

No. There is no joining fee or monthly charge. Commission is only deducted when a customer actually buys a pass and visits.

Can I pause availability?

Yes. Turn day passes on or off and control availability whenever you want — for example, during maintenance hours or peak seasons.
